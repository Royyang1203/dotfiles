## Theory of mind
Track other people's mental states.
::: boxes
- beliefs
- intentions
- desires
- knowledge
- emotions
:::

## The hard cases
Reading words at face value is not enough.
::: cards
- Irony | says one thing, means another
- Hints | the real request is unspoken
- Lies | track what they want you to think
- Misdirection | pull your attention elsewhere
:::
caption: All of these need theory of mind.

## Then came LLMs
- Pass some theory of mind tasks
- But results are mixed
- Break on tiny prompt changes
highlight: Shallow heuristics, **or robust theory of mind?**

# The study

## Study design
::: pillars
- Diverse tests | 5 tests | False belief, Irony, Faux pas, Hinting, Strange stories
- Repeated runs | 15× | sessions per model, per test
- Human baseline | 1,907 | humans — vs GPT-4, GPT-3.5, LLaMA2-70B
:::
caption: Each test targets a different ToM ability — scored against the human baseline.
